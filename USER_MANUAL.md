# StudentOS AI - User Manuals

## 1. Student Guide
- **Dashboard**: View your classes, AI recommendations, and weekly schedule.
- **Academics**: Track attendance, assignments, and calculate your predicted CGPA.
- **Fees & Results**: Pay outstanding dues and download PDF marksheets.
- **AI Assistant**: Click the floating robot icon to get instant help with your studies.

## 2. Faculty Guide
- **Dashboard**: View assigned subjects and class attendance trends.
- **Academics**: Mark attendance, grade exams, and assign tasks to students.
- **Analytics**: Identify at-risk students through the AI Performance Analysis.

## 3. Admin Guide
- **System Management**: Create and manage Student/Faculty accounts.
- **ERP Operations**: Schedule external exams, configure fee structures, and manage institutional departments.
- **Security**: Monitor the system via the Audit Logs and perform database Backup/Restores.
